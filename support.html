Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use Khabrr?

No. Khabrr works without any login or sign-up. Just pick your language, country, and topics, and you're in.

How do I change my language or topics?

Open the Settings tab in the app. You can update your language, country, and topics anytime.

What languages are supported?

Khabrr supports a wide range of languages, including Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu, Arabic, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Russian.

How does the Morning Bulletin work?

Each morning, Khabrr prepares an audio briefing of the day's top stories. Tap the 🎙 Bulletin button on any news card to listen hands-free.

Are my bookmarks saved if I reinstall?

Bookmarks are stored locally on your device, so they're removed if you uninstall the app. We're exploring optional sync for the future.

How is my privacy protected?

We collect no personal data. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
